# Special Lagnas — Classical Sources

## Overview

The six special lagnas supplement the primary Ascendant (Lagna). While Lagna
governs body and life direction, each special lagna governs a specific domain.

| Lagna | Sanskrit | Signifies | Primary Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ghatika | Gha-Ti-Ka | Power & authority | BPHS Ch. 5 |
| Hora | Ho-Ra | Wealth | BPHS Ch. 6, Brihat Jataka Ch. 3 |
| Bhava | Bha-Va | Vitality | BPHS Ch. 5 |
| Shree | Shree | Prosperity | Jaimini Sutras, Saravali |
| Indu | In-Du | Wealth potential | Jataka Parijata Ch. 12 |
| Pranapada | Pra-Na-Pa-Da | Life force | BPHS Ch. 5, Muhurta Chintamani Ch. 3 |
